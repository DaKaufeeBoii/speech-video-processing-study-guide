Pole–Zero Modeling of Speech Pole–zero modeling  is a fundamental technique in speech signal processing used to represent the  speech production system  as a digital filter.  It helps explain how raw sound from the vocal cords is shaped into intelligible speech.

Connecting to Pole–Zero Model

---

Poles and Zeros In digital signal processing, a system is represented by a transfer function: 𝐻 𝑧 = 𝐵 𝑧 𝐴 𝑧 = Zeros Poles B(z) →  Numerator  → gives  zeros   𝐴 𝑧 →  Denominator  → gives  poles   H(z)=Poles/ Zeros​  

---

Poles Poles 1. Represent resonances (formants) of the vocal tract  Resonance A  resonance  is a frequency where the system naturally vibrates strongly. In speech: The  vocal tract (throat + mouth cavity)  behaves like a  resonant tube   Certain frequencies get  amplified naturally  2.  Cause  peaks in the frequency spectrum   Poles come from the denominator: 𝐴 𝑧 =0⇒ Pole At these points →  𝐻 𝑧 becomes very large  This creates  peaks in frequency response Physical Interpretation  Air column inside the vocal tract vibrates  Shape of tongue, lips, jaw → changes cavity length  This changes  resonant frequencies (formants)  

---

Poles Why Poles Create Peaks? When frequency is near a pole: Denominator becomes very small  Output becomes large   Result:  sharp peak in spectrum Poles = Energy Concentration Points They  define vowel identity

---

Zeros 1. Represent  anti-resonances   An  anti-resonance  is a frequency where energy is  cancelled or blocked . Mathematical Meaning Zeros come from the numerator:  𝐵 𝑧 =0⇒ 𝐻 𝑧 =0 Output becomes zero at that frequency  Produces  deep dips (notches)    

---

Zeros Physical Interpretation Occurs when: Sound waves  interfere destructively   Or airflow splits (oral + nasal paths)   Especially important in: Nasal sounds  (/m/, /n/)  Some consonants  Example: Nasal Sound “/m/” Part of sound goes through  nose   Part through  mouth   These paths  interfere    Result: Some frequencies cancel →  zeros   Spectrum shows  dips (anti-formants) Why Zeros Create Dips? At zero: Numerator = 0  Output = 0   Result:  frequency completely suppressed Zeros = Energy Cancellation Points They  refine and modify speech quality

---

Physical Interpretation The  vocal tract (mouth, tongue, throat)  behaves like a tube  Different shapes → different resonances → different speech sounds  These resonances are mathematically  modeled  as  poles   Airflow restrictions (like nasal coupling) introduce  zeros

---

Why It Matters Pole-zero modeling is widely used in: 🎤 Speech synthesis (text-to-speech)  🧠 Speech recognition systems  📡 Speech compression (e.g., LPC codecs)  🎧 Voice transformation and enhancement

---

Simple Intuition Think of it like: Poles = Amplifiers (boost certain frequencies)   Zeros = Suppressors (reduce certain frequencies)   Together, they shape raw sound into recognizable speech.
