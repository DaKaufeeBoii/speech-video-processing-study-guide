
Speech & Video Processing — Question Bank
Unit 1
Discuss about the speech production mechanism. Identify the roles of excitation source, vocal tract system in speech generation.
Discrete time representation of speech signal and interpret the effects of sampling, signal reconstruction.
Analyze pole-zero modeling to represent the speech signals and computer system functions.
Analyze the properties of Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) relevant to the speech signal.
Discuss about the convolution operations in speech processing. Examine the rules in system response and filter mechanism.
Discuss the performance of linear and non-linear filter banks in speech signal decomposition with respect to frequency, resolution, and distortion.
Discuss the LPC, linear predictive coding equations, and evaluate its effectiveness in modeling speech signals for efficient parameter estimation.
Unit 2
Discuss the key stages of speech features extraction pipeline and the purpose of each stage in converting speech signal into feature representation.
Elaborately discuss about the role of static and dynamic features in speech processing.
Differentiate between LPCC and MFCC features in terms of spectral representation and robustness for each recognition application.
Analyze the different distance measures and their roles in speech pattern matching and recognition system.
Analyze the performance of vector models in speech recognition and justify codebook optimization strategy.
Discuss about GMM for speech feature distribution.
HMM model for continuous speech recognition and access its capability in modeling temporal variability of speech signal.

Unit 3
Discuss about the multidimensional signal processing framework for applications like images or video systems and justify the selected transforms and system models.
Discuss about the multidimensional system models by analyzing their input and output relationships using the convolution and transform domain methods.
Explain about the applications of multidimensional transform 2D DFT for analyzing signal representation in frequency domain.
Discuss the characteristics and representation of multidimensional signal and examine their significance in advanced signal processing applications.x
